# PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** September 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 25, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Maqhawe T Ngwenya** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles information in connection with **MM Music Player** ("App").

We designed MM Music Player primarily as an offline music application. Our goal is to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information and to keep music-library information on the user's device wherever technically possible.

By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the MM Music Player mobile, desktop, or other supported versions of the application.

It does not necessarily apply to third-party websites, applications, platforms, or services that may be accessible through or integrated with the App.

Those third parties may have their own privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how the App is designed and which features you use.

### 2.1 Information We May Not Collect

If the App operates entirely offline and does not require an account, we generally do not need to collect information such as:

* Your full name;
* Email address;

## 3. Music Library Information

To provide music-player functionality, the App may access information associated with audio files stored on your device.

This may include:

* Song title;
* Artist;
* Album;
* Genre;
* Track number;
* Duration;
* Release information;
* Album artwork;
* File format;
* File location;
* File size; and
* Other embedded metadata.

This information may be processed locally on your device to organize and display your music library.

## 4. Audio Files

The App may require permission to access audio files stored on your device.

This permission allows the App to identify and play compatible music files.

Unless a specific feature clearly states otherwise, we do not upload your personal music files to our servers.

Your music files remain under your control on your device.

## 5. Playlists and Favorites

If the App provides playlists, favourites, likes, queues, or similar features, related information may be stored locally on your device.

For example, the App may store:

* Which songs you added to a playlist;
* Which songs you marked as favourites;
* Playlist names;
* Playback order;
* Recently played tracks; and
* Playback preferences.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, this information is processed locally and is not transmitted to us.

## 6. Playback History

Depending on the features implemented in the App, we may store information about songs that you have played.

If playback history is stored locally, it is used to provide functionality such as:

* Playback statistics;
* Resume playback;
* Recently added music; or
* Personalized local organization.

We do not use locally stored playback history for advertising or behavioral profiling unless we explicitly disclose such use.
